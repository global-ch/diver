# Diving Resources

A comprehensive collection of external resources, organizations, and links for scuba diving education, training, and exploration.

## Certification Organizations

### Major Dive Certification Agencies
- **PADI (Professional Association of Diving Instructors)** - https://www.padi.com
  - Largest dive training organization worldwide
  - Multiple certification levels and specialties
  - Dive resort locator and training centers

- **SSI (Scuba Schools International)** - https://www.divessi.com
  - International certification and training
  - Specialties and continuing education
  - Digital certification cards

- **NAUI (National Association for Underwater Instruction)** - https://www.naui.org
  - Technical and recreational diving certifications
  - Instructor development programs
  - Dive center directory

- **CMAS (Confédération Mondiale des Activités Subaquatiques)** - https://www.cmas.org
  - World confederation of underwater activities
  - International standards and certifications
  - Multi-language support
