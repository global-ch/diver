# Diver - Scuba Diving Guide Repository

Welcome to the Diver repository! This is a comprehensive resource for scuba diving enthusiasts, beginners, and certified divers looking to expand their knowledge and skills.

## Overview

This repository contains educational materials, guides, and resources about scuba diving safety, techniques, certification, and marine exploration. Whether you're just starting your diving journey or looking to advance your skills, you'll find valuable information here.

## Contents

- **main.md** - Comprehensive scuba diving guide covering:
  - Getting started with scuba diving
  - Safety principles and procedures
  - Equipment overview
  - Marine life and environmental responsibility
  - Training and certification levels
  - Health considerations
  - Trip planning guidelines

## Getting Started

1. Read through the main guide to understand the basics
2. Check your local certification requirements
3. Take a certified scuba diving course
4. Start exploring the underwater world responsibly

## Safety First

Always remember:
- Never dive alone - use the buddy system
- Obtain proper certification before diving
- Follow all safety guidelines and depth limits
- Respect the marine environment
- Dive within your training level
- Maintain your equipment properly
